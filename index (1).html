IRON.LOG

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how IRON.LOG Gym Workout Tracker ("IRON.LOG", "the App") handles user and device information. The App is published under the developer name Veyaraki by Zsolt Kovács, Hungary. For privacy questions, contact veyarakidev@gmail.com.

At a glance: IRON.LOG is a local-first workout tracker. You do not need an account. Your workout history and settings are principally kept on your device, not uploaded to an IRON.LOG-operated server. Android system backups, files you deliberately export, Google Play reviews, support emails, and certain components in older app versions are separate data-handling situations described below. Version 1.0.1 (version code 10) does not package Google AdMob, its consent SDK, or Google Play Billing.

1. Workout and Fitness Information

IRON.LOG processes information you enter or generate while using the App, which may include workout plans, exercise names, custom exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, rest periods, dates, durations, cardio details, notes, workout history, personal records, statistics, and saved or unfinished workout sessions.

These records are held in an on-device SQLite database. No IRON.LOG account, login, or developer-operated workout-data server is required. The App uses this data to display and manage your workouts, history, summaries, progress, and recovery of unfinished sessions.

2. Local Settings and Preferences

IRON.LOG stores preferences and other application state locally, including training settings and information needed to resume the App. This information is used to remember your choices and keep features working between sessions.

3. Import, Export, and User-Selected Files

You can export an IRON.LOG JSON backup, import compatible JSON backups, and import supported CSV workout files, including compatible Hevy and Strong exports. Selected files are processed locally for import; the App does not send your workout database to a developer-operated server.

Exports may be written to your device's Downloads folder or to a location or document provider you select through Android. If you choose a cloud-backed location, or later share an exported file, that provider may store or transmit it under its own privacy policy. Keep exported workout files secure and delete copies you no longer need.

4. Android System Backup and Restore

IRON.LOG supports Android's operating-system backup and device-transfer mechanisms. Subject to Android version, device settings, backup-provider settings, eligibility, and quotas, a system backup may include the App's local workout database, saved sessions, preferences, and related files.

For version 1.0.1 (code 10), the App excludes the SQLite plugin's unused encrypted preferences file from Android cloud-backup and device-transfer rules to avoid restoring device-bound encrypted configuration without its original key. Android also excludes certain system-designated folders, such as cache and no-backup storage.

Android backup is provided by the operating system and its selected backup service (for example, Google), not an IRON.LOG cloud account. Backups are not continuous, and successful backup or restoration is not guaranteed. A recent workout may not yet have been backed up. The actual backup and restore behavior may vary by device and Android version. You can manage system backups in your Android device settings. Manual JSON exports offer an additional user-controlled backup option.

5. Advertising and In-App Purchases: Version Differences

Version 1.0.1 (version code 10): This release does not include the Google Mobile Ads/AdMob SDK, Google User Messaging Platform consent SDK, or Google Play Billing SDK. It has no active advertising or in-app purchase features, and the associated advertising-identifier and billing permissions are not packaged in this release.

Earlier releases, including version 1.0 (version code 9): Earlier Android packages included Google advertising and/or billing components, although advertising, consent, and purchase interfaces were disabled in the developer's release configuration. The presence of an SDK does not by itself establish that it transmitted data. Its actual runtime data transmission in those earlier releases has not been conclusively verified.

Where a Google Mobile Ads component is active and communicates with Google's services, Google states that data processed by the SDK can include IP-address-derived approximate location, interactions, diagnostics, performance data, and device or advertising identifiers for advertising, measurement, and fraud-prevention purposes. Google's handling of this information is governed by its applicable terms and Privacy Policy.

The Google Play store's Data safety information may cover practices of older releases still being distributed, even where a newer testing build has removed those components. If advertising or purchasing is introduced in a future release, this policy and the relevant store declarations will be updated.

6. Google Play Reviews and Ratings

IRON.LOG includes Google's Play In-App Review functionality. After certain app-use conditions are met, the App may request that Google Play display its rating or review dialog. The App uses workout counts and session identifiers locally to decide eligibility; these workout records are not passed to the review API as review content.

If you submit a rating or written review, Google Play processes your submission under Google's applicable privacy terms. You may also choose to open the App's Google Play listing directly.

7. Support Emails and External Links

When you choose "Report a Bug" or a support contact action, the App opens your email application with a draft message. It does not silently send a support report. If you send the email, we receive the email address, message, and any attachments or other details you choose to include. We use this information to respond, investigate reported issues, and maintain necessary support records. The applicable lawful basis, where the GDPR applies, is generally our legitimate interest in providing support or taking steps requested by you.

Links to this Privacy Policy, the Google Play listing, or other external pages open third-party services that may process information under their own terms. Visiting this policy page hosted through GitHub Pages may also involve routine website access information handled by GitHub under GitHub's privacy statement.

8. Permissions, SDKs, and Device Access

Version 1.0.1 retains permissions and platform capabilities associated with application operation, such as internet access and vibration, and uses Android's document-selection mechanisms when you import or export a file. Core workout logging is designed to work offline.

The App does not require access to precise GPS location, contacts, SMS messages, camera, or microphone for ordinary workout logging. Version 1.0.1 does not package a standalone Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Sentry, or similar analytics/crash-reporting client according to the available release audit. Standard Android or Google Play services may perform their own platform functions.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Local workout data and settings remain in the App's storage until you remove them using available App functions, clear the App's storage, or uninstall it. Uninstalling usually removes the App's local database, but separately saved JSON exports and eligible Android system backups may remain under the control of the user and the relevant storage or backup provider. Reinstalling the App does not guarantee recovery of previous data.

Because we do not operate an IRON.LOG account or server containing your workout history, we generally cannot access or remotely erase your local workout records. To remove an exported file, delete it from the storage location or cloud provider where you saved it.

Support emails are retained only as reasonably necessary to handle the inquiry and related follow-up, or where retention is required by applicable law. You can request deletion of personal information held in our support correspondence by emailing veyarakidev@gmail.com, subject to applicable legal exceptions.

10. Sharing, Selling, and Security

We do not sell your locally stored workout records. IRON.LOG does not upload your workout database to a developer-operated cloud service. Relevant third-party processing may arise from Android's system backup service, Google Play reviews, earlier-version SDK behavior described above, support email delivery, and external services or file destinations that you choose.

Keeping workout data on-device reduces routine remote exposure, but it does not eliminate risk. The workout database is not encrypted by an IRON.LOG-specific database encryption layer in the audited version; protection of local files also depends on Android's device security and your device settings. Protect your device and any manually exported files. No storage or transmission method is perfectly secure.

11. Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of, restriction of, or objection to processing of personal information that we hold, and in applicable circumstances data portability. You may contact us at veyarakidev@gmail.com. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection authority.

Most workout records remain on your device and are not accessible to us. Requests about data independently processed by Google, GitHub, your email provider, or another service may need to be directed to those providers.

12. Children's Privacy

IRON.LOG is a general fitness tracking utility and is not specifically directed at children under 13. It has no developer-operated account-registration service. If you believe you have sent a child's personal information to our support email, please contact us.

13. Fitness and Medical Disclaimer

IRON.LOG is a workout logging and fitness tracking tool, not a medical device. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice.

14. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functions, third-party integrations, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version and its revision date will be available at this same public URL.
