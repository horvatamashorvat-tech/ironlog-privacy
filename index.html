IRON.LOG Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

IRON.LOG (“the App”) is a workout tracking application provided by Veyaraki. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Workout and Fitness Data

IRON.LOG does not require you to create an account or sign in.

Information you enter into the App may include workout routines, exercise names, weights, repetitions, sets, rest times, workout dates and durations, cardio duration, distance and calories, custom exercises, notes, and workout history.

This workout and fitness information is primarily stored locally on your device using a local SQLite database. IRON.LOG does not automatically upload your workout history to servers operated by the developer.

2. Local App Preferences

Certain basic settings and technical information may also be stored locally on your device, such as application preferences and data used for migration from older versions of the App.

3. Backup, Export and Import

IRON.LOG allows you to export your workout information to a JSON backup file.

You may also import an IRON.LOG JSON backup or compatible workout data exported by other applications, such as Hevy or Strong CSV files.

Importing a file is initiated by you through the Android system file picker. IRON.LOG receives access only to the file that you choose.

Imported files are processed locally on your device and are not automatically sent to the developer.

Exported backup files are stored or shared according to the location and application you choose. If you upload or share a backup using Google Drive, email, Quick Share, or another third-party service, that service's own privacy practices apply.

4. Android System Backup

Android may back up certain application data as part of the device's system backup functionality, depending on your Android and Google account backup settings.

Such backups are controlled by the operating system and your device/account settings rather than by IRON.LOG.

5. Advertising

IRON.LOG uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK / Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information including IP address, app and advertisement interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and device or advertising identifiers.

Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and related purposes. Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

During development and closed testing, IRON.LOG may use Google's test advertisement units. Where legally required, appropriate consent mechanisms will be used before eligible personalized advertising is served.

Learn more about Google's privacy practices: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.

6. Third-Party Resources

IRON.LOG may load certain visual or technical resources from third-party services, including Google Fonts and Tenor-hosted exercise media.

When external resources are requested, the provider may receive technical request information such as your IP address and device/browser connection information. Their own privacy policies govern how they process such information.

7. Bug Reports and Contact

IRON.LOG includes a “Report a Bug” feature that opens your email application.

If you voluntarily contact the developer, the developer may receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any information or attachments that you choose to provide.

This information is used only to respond to your request, investigate problems, provide support, and improve the App.

8. Permissions

IRON.LOG uses internet access for functionality including advertising and externally hosted content.

The App does not require access to your precise GPS location, camera, microphone, contacts, or SMS messages for workout tracking.

File import is performed using Android's document selection system so that you explicitly choose which file the App may access.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Workout data stored locally remains on your device until you delete the relevant data, clear the App's storage, restore another backup, or uninstall the App, subject to any Android system backup that you have enabled.

Because the developer does not normally receive or store your workout history on developer-operated servers, the developer generally cannot access or remotely delete your locally stored workout data.

You can delete locally stored application data through the App where applicable or through Android's application storage settings.

If you have contacted the developer by email and would like your support correspondence deleted, you may request deletion using the contact address below.

Third-party services may retain information according to their own privacy and retention policies.

10. Security

IRON.LOG is designed to minimize unnecessary transmission of workout information by storing workout data locally. Files selected for import are accessed through Android's file access system.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Health and Medical Disclaimer

IRON.LOG is intended for workout tracking and general fitness purposes. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice.

12. Children's Privacy

IRON.LOG is not directed to children under the age of 13. The developer does not knowingly request personal information directly from children under 13.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the GDPR may provide rights regarding personal information held about you.

Most workout information in IRON.LOG is stored locally and is therefore under your direct control. For information that the developer actually holds, such as voluntary support emails, you may contact the developer to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when IRON.LOG's features, third-party services, or data practices change. The effective date shown at the top of this policy will be updated when material changes are made.
